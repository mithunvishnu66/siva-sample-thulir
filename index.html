Approach

Not a one-day plantation drive. A relationship with the land.

What began as Thenmozhi Mohankumar's passion for native plants is now a full working practice: sourcing rare and endangered flora, matching species to soil and rainfall, planting, and returning to maintain what was planted.

Every sapling leaves the nursery packed and identification-tagged — the detail clients mention most — so schools, companies, farms and civic authorities know exactly what goes where.
